Use the body for the full write-up: what the researchers asked, how they tested it, and what they found.

## Method

Describe who or what was studied, how many participants there were, and how the study was designed.

## Limitations

Note what the study cannot tell us, such as a small sample or a short follow-up period.

> A quote from the paper can be set apart like this.
